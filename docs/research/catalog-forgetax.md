# ForgeTax: relics and merchant items

Reference for [run-design.md](../run-design.md). Effects as listed by [ForgeTax.tools](https://www.forgetax.tools/relics), an unofficial fan database worked out from the game's code (Oct 2026); the game and its data belong to GraveYardDev.

**98 relics** on an 8-slot shelf: 82 from the relic shop (paid in sword credits, from day 2), 8 masterworks (forge a family to +20) and 8 achievement relics. The site sorts them into 10 effect families:

Economy 15, Success 13, Hold 11, On break 10, Frenzy and speed 10, Damage 10, Break protection 8, Great 8, Streaks and patterns 7, Sword value 6.
